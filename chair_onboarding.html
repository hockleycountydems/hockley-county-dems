You're a Precinct Chair... Now What?

Welcome to the team! Being a Precinct Chair is the most important grassroots position in the Democratic Party. It can feel overwhelming at first, but remember: "eat the elephant one bite at a time." You don't need to do everything today. Choose a path below to start exploring your role and building your community.

Need some inspiration?

Wondering what a highly successful Precinct Chair actually looks like in practice? Read our deep-dive case study on Precinct 107. Discover how one Chair organized a coalition, threw community events, connected with the Mayor, and turned out hundreds of voters.
